The problem we're solving

Stokvels and savings clubs are a trusted way for communities to save. Many groups still run on spreadsheets, notebooks and WhatsApp messages.

Stokvel accounts offered by individual banks need every member to bank with the same institution. That leaves out members who bank elsewhere.

JamiPay handles the mechanics of the group (members, recurring contributions, rotating payouts and a clear record of every transaction) so a group can run one shared pool across banks.

How it works

  1. Create a group

    Set the contribution amount, how often members pay, and the payout order.

  2. Add members

    Invite people to join, whichever bank they use.

  3. Contribute each cycle

    Contributions are tracked, so everyone can see who has paid.

  4. Pay out in turn

    Each cycle the pool goes to the next member in the rotation.

Where we are

  • JamiPay is in early development.
  • We're preparing to pilot with a small number of savings groups (around 3 to 5 groups of 5 to 10 members).
  • We are not processing live payments yet. Before we do, we'll put the right account and compliance arrangements in place.
