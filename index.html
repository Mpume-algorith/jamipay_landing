The problem we're solving

How it works

  1. Create a group

    Set the contribution amount, how often members pay, and the payout order.

  2. Add members

    Invite people to join, whichever bank they use.

  3. Contribute each cycle

    Contributions are tracked, so everyone can see who has paid.

  4. Pay out in turn

    Each cycle the pool goes to the next member in the rotation.
